# Original Bruma Café imagery

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool. The selected images are hosted locally by the app, with WebP encoding for delivery; their composition has not been changed.

- Hero: `apps/web/public/images/roasting-hero.webp`
- Product photograph: `apps/web/public/images/coffee-bag.webp`

The product photograph is a deliberately unbranded kraft bag. Accessible HTML labels provide each coffee's name and origin over the photograph, allowing translated product information without embedding translations in raster images. Five label colours distinguish the five coffees.

## Hero prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium coffee brand website hero photograph, vertical 4:5 composition. Create an original editorial photograph: a weathered bronze coffee scoop filled with freshly roasted warm brown coffee beans, held by one artisan hand entering from the top left, suspended over an open dark coffee roasting cooling drum. Very close tactile detail, soft steam, diagonal bands of late afternoon light and shadow across a deep forest green charcoal background, warm copper highlights. Elevated European coffee magazine photography, quiet and authentic, cinematic chiaroscuro with visible subtle grain, real imperfect materials. Main scoop and beans centered in upper-middle. Leave lower quarter dark and clear, NO typography, NO logos, no rendered interface or collage. The photograph must feel dark, luxurious and photographic, not an illustration. Crop suitable for both desktop and mobile.

## Product prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: reusable coffee packaging photograph for product catalogue. Create a photorealistic premium studio photograph, vertical 4:5, of one unbranded natural kraft-paper 250g coffee bag with folded top, standing perfectly upright, front toward camera at a very subtle three-quarter angle, on a rough dark volcanic stone pedestal. The front face has absolutely no label, no print or decoration, so we can place an accessible HTML product label over its central lower section. The bag centered, occupying 65% of image height, fully within frame, with plenty of negative space around it. Matte charcoal forest-green background #20251e, soft left side daylight, beautifully detailed paper folds, realistic gentle shadow and warm kraft color, luxury independent coffee roaster campaign, organic natural photographic quality. No coffee cup, no beans floating, no writing, no logo, no watermark, no UI. The bag has a broad flat central front face.

## Code-native identity

The coffee-bean mark, wordmark composition and favicon are SVG and HTML assets maintained with the UI. Fonts are bundled through Fontsource, without runtime font requests to third-party services.
